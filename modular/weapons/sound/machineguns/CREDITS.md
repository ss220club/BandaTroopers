# Machine gun audio

- `mg42_fire.wav`: adapted from [Authentic M60 Firing](https://freesound.org/people/FranklinRook1984/sounds/538302/) by FranklinRook1984, [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). A single shot was cut from the live-fire recording and faded for repeated playback.
- `mg3_fire.wav`: adapted from [M4 Assault Rifle Firing](https://freesound.org/people/mnslugger20/sounds/259758/) by mnslugger20, [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). A single shot was cut from the range-recorded burst, reduced by 3 dB, and faded for repeated playback.
